Welcome to the Delegation Experiment

Your role: {{ player.clean_role }} Manager

Overview

You will be assigned to manage a {{ player.clean_role }} company.

Your only task is to delegate one negotiation to either a {{ agent_1 }} or an {{ agent_2 }}.

Key Information
  • The expected negotiation performance of the {{ agent_1 }} and the {{ agent_2 }} is equal.
  • {{ agent_1 }} negotiators are augmented with the same optimal offers as the {{ agent_2 }}.
  • Base Delegation Costs:
    • Delegating to a {{ agent_1 }} costs 20€.
    • Delegating to the {{ agent_2 }} costs 10€.
  • Each participant will be assigned one of three product classes (A, B, or C), which determines the Transaction Cost (TC).
  • TC has a 50% probability of being halved if both parties delegate to {{ agent_1 }}s.
  • There is one upcoming negotiation, thus one delegation decision on behalf of your organization.
  • The negotiation will be conducted right after both parties make their delegation decision.
Negotiation Context

Negotiations will be held after the delegation decision. Retailers and Suppliers negotiate via text on the wholesale price & quantity of Pellet bags.

  • The item is produced by the supplying company at a Production Cost (PC) of 3, 4, or 5.
  • This item is sold by the retailer company at a Retail Price (RP) of 11.
  • PC and RP are common knowledge and are randomly drawn with equal probability.
  • Demand will be randomly drawn as 0 or 100 with equal probability right after the negotiation.
  • Suppliers carry the risk of unsold inventory (any quantity > demand will expire).
Your Choice

  • Choosing the {{ agent_2 }} signals a focus on extracting transactional value.
  • Choosing a {{ agent_1 }} signals an intent to build a relationship.
  • If both parties delegate to {{ agent_1 }}s, there is a 50% chance of building a relationship.
  • This relationship grants a huge reduction in Transaction Costs (reduced by half).
  • This potential cut in TC arises from the relationship of trust built through {{ contact }} contact.

Cost Calculations

Assuming both parties delegate to a {{ agent_1 }}, here is how to calculate the expected costs:

Expected delegation cost to {{ agent_1 }} (conditional on facing a {{ agent_1 }} counterpart):
Expected({{ agent_1 }} Cost) = 0.5 × (TC) + 0.5 × 0.5 × (TC) + 20 = 0.75 × (TC) + 20

Example (Product Class A):

  • Expected({{ agent_1 }} Cost) = 0.75 × 50 + 20 = 57.50€
  • {{ agent_2 }} Cost = TC + 10 = 50 + 10 = 60€

However, when the counterpart delegates to an {{ agent_2 }}, costs are certain:

Delegation cost to {{ agent_1 }} (conditional on facing a {{ agent_2 }} counterpart):
{{ agent_1 }} to {{ agent_2 }} Cost = TC + 20

Example (Product Class A):

  • {{ agent_1 }} Cost = 50 + 20 = 70€
  • {{ agent_2 }} Cost = 50 + 10 = 60€

    • {% endif %}

Your compensation is determined by the company profits.

You will get 2.5% of the company profits as a bonus.

For example, if the company profits are 100€, you will get 2.5€ as a bonus.

Company Profits = Profits from Deal after Demand is Known – Delegation Cost – Bonus Delegee

The negotiations will be conducted right after both parties make their delegation decision.

After the negotiation is conducted, demand will be randomly drawn and you will be informed of your monetary compensation.
