Overview

In this exercise, you will take the role of an employee at {% player.clean_opposite_role %} company that negotiates over bags of pellets.

  • {% if agent_1 == 'Senior Agent' %} Both companies employ human agents to negotiate over bags of pellets {%else %} Both companies employ AI Agents and humans to negotiate over bags of pellets {% endif %}
  • The item is produced by the supplying company at a Production Cost (PC) of {% production_costs %}
  • This item is sold by the retailer company at a Retail Price (RP) of {% market_price %}
  • The Retailer and Supplier negotiate via chat on Wholesale Price (w) & Quantity (q)
  • PC & RP are common knowledge and PC is drawn with equal probability.
  • After the negotiation, Demand (D) will be randomly drawn {% demand %} (Equal Probability)
  • Supplier carries the risk of unsold inventory (any q > Demand will expire)
    • {% if agent_1 == 'Senior Agent' %}
  • Your manager will choose to delegate an upcoming negotiation to you
  • You will negotiate and be financially compensated for the outcome
    • {%else %}

  • If the manager delegates it to the AI Agent, you will not negotiate and be Idle, but still be financially compensated for the outcome of the AI Agent
    • {% endif %}

For the rest of the experiment, you will be employed by the {% player.clean_role %} company.

Objective

Your goal is to agree on Wholesale Price (w) and Quantity (q) maximizing your profits.


Terms of Negotiation
  • Quantity (q) Ranges from {% C.DEMAND_MIN %} to {% C.DEMAND_MAX %} without decimals
  • Wholesale Price (w) Denoted in euros (€) with two decimals

Roles and Profit Calculation

Supplier Profit = w * min(q, D) - PC * q
Example: Your Production Cost (PC) is 4€, and you just agreed with the retailer on a wholesale price (w) of 7€ and a quantity (q) of 50:

  • Demand (D) is 75: 7 * min(50, 75) - 4 * 50 = 150€
  • Demand (D) is 25: 7 * min(50, 25) - 4 * 50 = - 25€
  • Demand (D) is 50: 7 * min(50, 50) - 4 * 50 = 150€

Retailer Profit = RP - w * min (q, D)
Example: Your Retail Price (RP) is 11€, and you just agreed with the retailer on a wholesale price (w) of 7€ and a quantity (q) of 50:

  • Demand (D) is 75: (11-7) * min(50, 75) = 200€
  • Demand (D) is 25: (11-7) * min(50, 25) = 100€
  • Demand (D) is 50: (11-7) * min(50, 50) = 200€
